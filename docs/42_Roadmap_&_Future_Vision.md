# AI-Powered Petrochemical Trading & Enterprise Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

Build the world's leading AI-powered petrochemical trading and enterprise operating system.

The platform will evolve from an internal enterprise solution into a globally deployable, extensible, AI-native business platform supporting petrochemical trading, manufacturing, logistics, energy, and adjacent industries.

- Eliminate repetitive manual work
- Enable AI-assisted decision making
- Unify enterprise operations
- Deliver real-time business intelligence
- Provide an extensible platform for future growth

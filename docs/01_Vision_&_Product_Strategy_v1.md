# 01_Vision_&_Product_Strategy.md

## AI-Powered Petrochemical Trading & Enterprise Platform

### Vision
Build an AI-first Enterprise Operating System purpose-built for petrochemical trading companies. AI performs operational work while people supervise, approve exceptions, and make strategic decisions.

## Mission
Create one intelligent platform combining:
- AI
- Product Information Management
- Trading
- CRM
- Accounting
- HR
- Inventory
- Procurement
- Knowledge Management

## Core Principles
- AI First
- Multi-Tenant
- Plugin-Based
- Modular
- API First
- Security by Design
- Cloud Ready
- Future Scalable

# 00_Project_Overview.md

## AI-Powered Petrochemical Trading & Enterprise Platform

**Version:** 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

This platform is not a traditional CRM.

It is an **AI-First Enterprise Operating System (AI EOS)** purpose-built
for petrochemical trading companies.

The AI acts as the primary operator. Employees supervise, approve
exceptions, and define business policies.

The platform supports multiple independent companies (multi-tenant)
while keeping all business data isolated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

Create a single platform that automates end-to-end business operations
for petrochemical trading companies, reducing manual work while
improving speed, consistency, and decision making.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Domain

Primary industries:

-   Petrochemical Trading
-   Base Oils
-   Bitumen
-   Lubricants
-   Industrial Chemicals
-   Additives
-   Specialty Chemicals
-   Future product categories

The product catalog must be dynamic. New categories, specifications, and
documents should be configurable without code changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Goals

-   Replace repetitive manual work with AI.
-   Centralize company knowledge.
-   Standardize trading workflows.
-   Support international trade.
-   Scale from one company to many companies.
-   Enable future SaaS deployment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI-First Philosophy

Every feature must answer one question:

> Can AI complete this task automatically?

If yes:

-   AI performs the task.
-   Human reviews only when company policy requires approval.

Examples:

-   Customer qualification
-   Product recommendation
-   Quotation creation
-   Export document preparation
-   Invoice generation
-   Payment reminders
-   Knowledge search
-   Email drafting
-   WhatsApp conversations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Human Responsibilities

Humans should focus on:

-   Strategic negotiations
-   Pricing policies
-   Large discount approvals
-   Financial approvals
-   Regulatory compliance
-   Customer relationship management for exceptional cases

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Primary Users

## Platform Owner

Owns the software platform.

## Company Administrator

Configures company settings, users, branding, permissions, and AI
policies.

## Manager

Approves workflows that exceed policy thresholds.

## Employee

Reviews AI recommendations and handles exceptions.

## Customer

Interacts through WhatsApp, email, portal, or desktop support.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Multi-Tenant Platform

One platform.

Unlimited companies.

Each company has isolated:

-   Users
-   Products
-   Customers
-   Quotations
-   Orders
-   Documents
-   AI Knowledge
-   Branding
-   Financial data

No cross-company data access.
